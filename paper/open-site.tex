\begin{abstract}
A step is already ordinal, so a metaphysics that takes acts as primitive
gets time almost free and has nowhere for extension to come from. This
note starts from which determinations can be settled together. What
holds, holds together: actuality is coherent partial settlement; space
is independence and interaction in that coherence; time is the ancestral
of articulative growth, kept apart from revision; the possible is unused
coherence. Lean~4 checks the load-bearing claims in
\texttt{OpenSite.lean}.
\end{abstract}

\section{The diagnosis}

The binary latent--actual development, and the first draft of Open Sites,
both took a step as primitive. Seed, cadence, offer, rank, and
\lean{Continues.persists} are elaborations of that sequential peg.
\lean{Continues.persists} stipulated that an act disturbs one kind, which
is exactly the clause that prevents spatial content: nothing in the
primitive said which determinations constrain which.

The test is: for each target notion, complete ``$X$ would be ---, in
terms of the primitives.'' If the sentence has no completion, the notion
will not be derived. Under an act-first schema, ``space would be ---''
has none. Under compossibility it completes: space is the structure of
what can be settled together.

The binary file is not retracted. It remains the right special case for
promote-and-turn on dichotomous kinds. This note is the successor schema
that can hold the rest of the stated ceiling.

\section{Ceiling}

What holds, holds together. A configuration is an admissible partial
assignment of values to kinds, together with an articulation predicate.
Actuality, in this thin sense, is that coherent partial settlement ---
not a second dynamics, and not a focus that picks which kind to settle.

Space is independence and interaction in the pairwise compatibility
relation. Locality is amalgamation of independent settlements, a
theorem. Time is the ancestral of articulative growth. Oscillation is
revision of a value at fixed articulation. Those are two structures, so
the arrow and the swirl do not compete for one relation. The possible is
unused coherence: every coherent settle, grow, or revise. Observation is
restriction to a region of kinds.

Out of scope, on purpose: dimension, metric, signature, phenomenal
duration, embodiment, consciousness, being-from-nothing, and
$\omega$-history from seriality.

\section{The schema}

\begin{definition}[Ontology]
Kinds are types of values. Exclusivity is optional. Binary opposition is
a property of some kinds, not the shape of the field.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Coherence]
A pairwise compatibility relation on determinations, reflexive and
symmetric, and irreflexive on distinct values of an exclusive kind.
Higher-order (Borromean) constraints are declined: with those, locality
would again be a stipulation.
\end{definition}

A configuration is an admissible partial assignment together with an
articulation predicate. Latency is already threefold: settled for,
settled against (exclusive contrary), or unset. Articulation accumulates;
values vary.

\begin{formalresult}[Kind status]
Undrawn, open, and settled are pairwise exclusive
(\lean{settled\_not\_open}, \lean{settled\_not\_undrawn},
\lean{open\_not\_undrawn}). They are exhaustive when articulation is
decidable (\lean{kind\_status}). On an exclusive kind, a contrary is
excluded and incompatible (\lean{exclusive\_excludes}). That is
synchronic: it does not freeze the cell.
\end{formalresult}

\begin{definition}[Independence]
Kinds $k$ and $\ell$ are independent when every pair of values is
compatible.
\end{definition}

\begin{formalresult}[Locality is a theorem]
If $k$ and $\ell$ are independent and both are open at $\sigma$, then
coherent settlements of each amalgamate to a coherent joint settlement,
the two orders of update agree, and what is settable at $\ell$ is
unchanged by settling $k$. Three pairwise-independent settlements
amalgamate by iterating the pair theorem.
\end{formalresult}

This is \lean{independent\_amalgamate}, \lean{independent\_commute},
\lean{independent\_settable\_iff}, \lean{independent\_settle\_diamond},
and \lean{independent\_amalgamate3}. The world model witnesses the
complement: switch-off and lamp-on are each admissible and jointly not
(\lean{interaction\_blocks\_amalgamation}). Space here constrains what
can happen. It is adjacency and independence, not dimension or metric.

\begin{definition}[Passages]
Settling fills an open kind. Growing articulates a new kind, same
assignment. Revising changes a value, same articulation. Time
(\lean{Earlier}) is the ancestral of growth only.
\end{definition}

\begin{formalresult}[The arrow is strict]
\lean{Earlier} is irreflexive and asymmetric. Settling and revising do
not enter it: they keep articulation fixed, so they are not growth.
\end{formalresult}

This is \lean{earlier\_irrefl}, \lean{earlier\_asymm},
\lean{settles\_not\_earlier}, \lean{revises\_not\_earlier}. The world
model witnesses a genuine step: the seed (only the gap articulated)
grows to the origin (\lean{earlier\_seed\_origin}), and the origin is
not earlier than itself or than the seed.

\begin{formalresult}[Revision is replacement]
\lean{revise} overwrites a settled cell. Exclusivity does not block the
new value: \lean{settable\_overwrite} is the diachronic fact. The world
model revises hue from red to green, and that step is not
\lean{Earlier} (\lean{World.hue\_oscillates}).
\end{formalresult}

\begin{formalresult}[Possible is unused coherence]
\lean{Possible} is coherent settle, grow, or revise. An open settable
kind has a settle; an undrawn kind has a grow; a settled settable kind
has a revise. Coherence alone does not yield a successor
(\lean{productivity\_is\_not\_coherence\_level}): the inert passage
relation is empty. A selector orients values, not which kind is
settled; two settable values at an open kind exceed any such selector
(\lean{plenitude\_exceeds\_selector}).
\end{formalresult}

\begin{formalresult}[Restriction is a sub-configuration]
A region is a predicate on kinds. \lean{Config.restrict} keeps
assignment and articulation on the region and hides the rest. That is
veridical access, not consciousness. Illusion is a further primitive
and is not derived from restriction. The saturated world, restricted to
hue and lamp, keeps those values and hides switch and gap
(\lean{World.seen\_hides\_saturated}).
\end{formalresult}

\begin{formalresult}[Remainder is optional]
Pairwise compatibility does not force an open cell. A one-kind closed
configuration has none (\lean{remainder\_not\_coherence\_level}); the
same world that is open at the origin can be fully settled
(\lean{World.world\_not\_remainderLaw}). Permanent latency is
\lean{RemainderLaw}, not a theorem. What \emph{is} a theorem: settling
one open kind leaves a different open kind open
(\lean{settle\_preserves\_other\_open}), and growing an undrawn kind
creates remainder (\lean{grow\_remainder}).
\end{formalresult}

\begin{formalresult}[Independence is not forced]
A two-kind interacting coherence has no independent pair
(\lean{independence\_not\_forced}). Space as independence is a structure
some coherences have. The world model has both: hue is independent of
switch, lamp, and gap; switch-off and lamp-on refuse to amalgamate.
\end{formalresult}

Binary opposition is recovered where it holds. On a dichotomous kind,
settlement orients one value and excludes the other
(\lean{dichotomous\_orients}); hue, with three values, is not
dichotomous.

\section{Premises}

The load-bearing assumption is pairwise \lean{compat}. Everything else
in the ceiling is either a definition, a theorem, or an optional law.

\small

  >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.32\linewidth}
  >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.32\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Item & Status & Role \\
\midrule
\lean{compat}
  & primitive (refl, symm; exclusive irreflexive)
  & which determinations can be settled together \\
\lean{Exclusive}, \lean{Dichotomous}
  & optional properties of kinds
  & synchronic exclusion; two-valued orientation \\
\lean{RemainderLaw}
  & optional
  & permanent open cell; not a theorem of coherence \\
\lean{Selector}
  & optional, thin
  & orients values; does not pick the kind \\

  & theorems or definitions
  & amalgamation; ancestral of growth; unused coherence \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Plenitude, in the binary development, was an extra maximality axiom.
Here the constructors already supply the witnesses. Counting those
witnesses as \lean{Possible} is the one existential; it is not a second
continuation relation that might be a proper subset.

\section{Models}

Four models keep the optional claims honest.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \lean{Void}: null assignment, remainder. Presentation structure
        does not create actuality
        (\lean{nullity\_not\_derivable}).
  \item \lean{Closed}: one settled exclusive kind, no remainder.
        Permanent latency is not a theorem of coherence.
  \item \lean{Locked}: switch and lamp only, no independent pair.
        Independence is not a consequence of having kinds.
  \item \lean{World}: hue, gap, switch, and lamp. Independent triples
        amalgamate; switch-off and lamp-on do not. The origin is open;
        a saturated assignment of the same kinds is not. Hue revises
        without advancing \lean{Earlier}. Restriction to hue and lamp
        hides the switch.
\end{itemize}

\section{Objections}

The binary paper answered thirteen objections in a flat list. Several
are retired by the schema change (binary-only field, cadence as time,
swirl-as-return, internally marked past). The rest group by what they
target.

\subsection{Method}

\paragraph{The conclusions are stipulated in the premises.}
Some are. Pairwise compatibility is a primitive. \lean{RemainderLaw} and
a selector, if used, are optional. The reply is a dependency audit, not
a claim that Lean makes the primitives self-evident. The audit is
stricter than in the binary file: locality is a theorem, not
\lean{Continues.persists}; remainder and independence are not theorems
of having a coherence; \lean{Possible} is packed from constructors
rather than postulated as a maximal continuation relation.

\paragraph{The models are artificial.}
They are, in the proper sense of model constructions. Their role is to
witness joint satisfiability and to catch packages that do not follow:
nullity at presentation strength, remainder as a law, independence as a
law. They are not empirical evidence and not a unique intended
interpretation.

\subsection{The field}

\paragraph{Binary opposition is metaphysically reductive.}
The binary file granted this: it stated what follows in the two-pole
case. Here kinds are types of values. Dichotomy is a property some
kinds have (\lean{World.dichotomous\_sw}) and hue does not
(\lean{World.not\_dichotomous\_hue}). The reductive charge against the
field as a whole no longer applies. A still richer account of
continuous or dispositional determinables would need more than types of
values; that is declined, not smuggled.

\paragraph{Contrary determination has been confused with logical
negation.}
The types still prevent this. A value of an exclusive kind is not the
negation of a proposition. Realization exclusivity relates them:
distinct values of an exclusive kind are incompatible
(\lean{exclusive\_incompat}), so at most one is assigned. That is ontic
contrariety, not $\neg$.

\paragraph{Pairwise compatibility is too weak; the world has
Borromean constraints.}
It is too weak for those constraints, and that is the point. With
higher-order incompatibility, amalgamation of independent regions would
again be a stipulation. Pairwise compatibility is the weakest primitive
from which locality is a theorem. A metaphysics that needs Borromean
structure must add it and give up that derivation.

\subsection{Latency and possibility}

\paragraph{Latency is merely a negative predicate.}
At the broadest level, unset is non-assignment. The theory refines it
into exclusion, openness, and undrawnness, pairwise exclusive, with
synchronic exclusion of the contrary on exclusive kinds. What it does
not supply is an intensional theory of powers or degrees. That remains
an extension point.

\paragraph{Plenitude is too strong.}
In the binary file, maximality was extensionally the full lawful
continuation relation, and the objection was well founded against
presenting that as weak. Here there is no such axiom. Open, undrawn,
and settled unused coherence each have a constructor. The remaining
strength is the decision to count those constructors as
\lean{Possible}, and the selector-excess theorem, which still needs two
settable values at one open kind.

\paragraph{The non-nullity argument is circular.}
There is no non-nullity argument. \lean{Void} shows that a coherent
null configuration with remainder is admissible.
\lean{RemainderLaw} is optional; \lean{Closed} and a saturated
\lean{World} show it can fail. The valid claim is never creation of
actuality from absolute nullity.

\subsection{Time}

\paragraph{The archive simply stipulates the arrow of time.}
\lean{Earlier} is defined as the ancestral of articulative growth.
Irreflexivity and asymmetry are theorems of that definition, not of
temporally neutral dynamics. The theory explains no-return along growth
by a precise record principle (articulation strictly enlarges). It does
not derive an arrow from laws that do not mention growth.

\paragraph{Recurrence is not return.}
Along \lean{Earlier}, yes: a growth chain cannot return to its source.
At fixed articulation, no: \lean{revise} can restore a previous value,
and that step is not earlier. The binary swirl used fresh trace
carriage to keep qualitative reversal from being identity of
presentations. This schema has no traces. The honest slogan is
narrower: growth does not return; revision at fixed articulation can.

\paragraph{This is not lived time.}
Correct. There is no cadence, duration, tempo, anticipation, affect, or
embodiment. There is also no internally marked past: trace encoding was
a binary-file premise, declined here rather than re-stipulated.
\lean{Earlier} is a strict partial order generated by growth, not an
ordinal, not $\omega$-history, and not a formal present or future.

\subsection{Space}

\paragraph{Independence is not space.}
It is not dimension, metric, or signature. It is the product structure
of the coherence complex: which kinds constrain which. A coherence
complex has a shape; nothing in the primitives makes that shape
three-dimensional. Causal set theory has been stuck at the analogous
point with more machinery. Adjacency and commuting updates, yes;
relativistic spacetime, no.

\paragraph{Independence is smuggled as a law of kinds.}
It is not. \lean{Locked} has two kinds and no independent pair.
Independence is a structure some coherences have.

\subsection{Actuality and observation}

\paragraph{A selector makes determinacy trivial.}
The selector only orients values. Which kind is settled remains a
choice. Even the value-orientation is exceeded by two settable values
at one open kind. There is no focus, no cadence, and no unique
successor configuration.

\paragraph{Restriction does not explain experience.}
It does not. \lean{Config.restrict} is veridical access to a region of
kinds. It does not supply consciousness, error, salience, or phenomenal
character. Illusion is a further primitive; identifying observation
with restriction so tightly that illusion becomes inexpressible was
rejected in the diagnosis.

\section{Scope}

Adjacency, independence, commuting updates, and a strict order of
articulative growth, yes. Dimension, metric, signature, phenomenal
duration, embodiment, and $\omega$-history, no. A construction that
succeeds at a stated target beats one that half-succeeds at an unstated
one.

What is carried over from the binary project: unlabelled (now un-$n$-ary)
kinds, unused coherence as the unique existential, internal articulation
from the start, and the impossibility results as design constraints.
What is rejected: a binary-only field; identifying observation with
restriction so tightly that illusion becomes inexpressible; claiming
metric structure; treating remainder, independence, or seriality as
theorems of coherence.
