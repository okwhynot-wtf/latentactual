\begin{abstract}
The binary latent--actual development proved a coherent package, then asked
that package to be time, space, causation, observation, and continuous
determination as well. Those are different jobs. This note proposes a
successor schema whose primitives match the distinctions type theory can
check: kinds as types of values, sites with remainder, acts that settle a
value, time as the ranked ancestral of acts, coexistence as joint actuality,
causation as one-step acting, and appearance that is not realisation. A Lean
4 file, \texttt{OpenSite.lean}, checks the core theorems, a void
countermodel, and a world with three-valued hue, two places, reserved
remainder, illusion, and a supplied $\omega$-history.
\end{abstract}

\section{The diagnosis}

The binary field was a good engine for one question: can local determinacy
coexist with permanent latency, qualitative reversal, and modal excess over
selection? It answered that question. It is a poor engine for every
neighbouring question, because almost every headline theorem used unique
contrariety --- \lean{flip}, \lean{pole\_dichotomy}, promote-then-turn,
$e=\overline{d}$.

Generalising the field while keeping those theorems is not a lemma. Encoding
time in traces, space in archive incomparability, and observation in
co-realisation produced names that outran their content. The honest move is
not a wider binary schema. It is a stratified one.

\paragraph{Do not encode. Stratify.}
Give each metaphysical job its own primitive. Prove only what that primitive
yields. When a further phenomenon is wanted, add a further primitive, not a
pun.

\section{The schema}

\begin{definition}[Ontology]
An ontology supplies a type of kinds, a value type for each kind, and a
predicate \emph{exclusive} on kinds. A determinate is a value of a kind.
Exclusivity is optional: exclusive kinds realise at most one value at a
site; non-exclusive kinds may realise several.
\end{definition}

Binary opposition is a \emph{property of a kind}
(\lean{Ontology.Dichotomous}), not the shape of the cosmos. A hue with three
values is first-class. A continuous kind is a kind whose value type is
whatever ordered magnitude one later supplies. The schema does not construct
the reals; it also does not forbid them.

\begin{definition}[Site]
A site articulates kinds and realises values. Realisation implies
articulation. Exclusive kinds realise at most one value. Remainder is a site
law: every site has an articulated kind with no realised value.
\end{definition}

Remainder is not a theorem of the kind/value distinction. The void model
satisfies remainder while realising nothing. Actuality is not derived from
presentation.

\begin{definition}[Act]
An act from $s$ to $t$ settles one value at one kind, preserves realisation
at every other kind, and does not delete prior articulation. Dynamics add a
rank that every act advances.
\end{definition}

The rank is an honest historicity premise --- a clock, not a memory and not
a phenomenology. Without it, ancestry need not be acyclic. With it, the
ancestral of acts is a strict order.

\begin{formalresult}[Time as ancestry]
\lean{Earlier} is irreflexive, transitive, and asymmetric.
Off-target realised content persists through one act.
\end{formalresult}

\begin{formalresult}[Productivity is not completed succession]
Seriality (\lean{Productive}) yields arbitrarily long finite chains, as a
propositional existence. A completed $\omega$-history is extra data: a
function $\mathbb{N}\to\mathrm{Carrier}$ with a step at every index. The
abstract implication from seriality to such a function is countable
dependent choice, which the file does not assume. The world model
\emph{supplies} a history.
\end{formalresult}

This is the one old open problem that was already a missing theorem rather
than a missing phenomenon. It is now proved, and the two notions are not
identified.

\section{Selection, space, cause, appearance}

If an open kind has two values and both are possible targets, no selector is
respected (\lean{richAt\_exceeds\_selector}). Plenitude is local to a
workable kind. Remainder may be a reserved, unsettleable kind. The world
model uses a gap of that form, so remainder and modal excess do not fight.

\begin{definition}[Coexistence]
A coexistence relation is reflexive and symmetric, and incompatible with
\lean{Earlier}.
\end{definition}

Joint actuality is the spatial primitive. It is not geometry. It is also not
archive incomparability: two possible successors of one site are not thereby
co-present. The world model has two locations at clock zero that are
together and not temporally ordered.

\begin{definition}[Direct cause]
$s$ directly causes $t$ when some act runs from $s$ to $t$.
\end{definition}

Every direct cause is earlier. The converse fails on a two-step run: the
endpoint is later, not a one-step successor. Causation is not identified
with temporal precedence, and the distinction uses no extra causal
primitive beyond the act itself. Counterfactuals and prehension remain
outside.

\begin{definition}[Appearance]
An observer-relation of appearing is not required to coincide with
realisation. Veridical appearance is the conjunction; illusion is appearance
without realisation.
\end{definition}

The old observer layer forbade illusion by co-realisation. Here illusion is
inhabited in the world model (a low pulse that appears high). Location of
an observer is a constraint on appearance, not a lived body.

\section{What is closed, and what is not}

Relative to the old remaining-work paragraph:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.42\linewidth}p{0.52\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Old omission & Status in Open Sites \\
\midrule
Plural determination & Closed as kinds with $n$ values; hue is trichotomous. \\
Productivity vs $\omega$-history & Closed as two theorems, not identified. \\
Formal future & The converse of \lean{Earlier}; not marked at the source. \\
Space & Coexistence, disjoint from time; not geometry. \\
Causation & Direct act vs ancestry; not a causal calculus. \\
Observation & Appearance, with illusion; not consciousness. \\
Continuous / metric time & Still outside (needs an ordered magnitude). \\
Phenomenal duration, embodiment & Still outside (would be further primitives). \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The last items are outside because they need further primitives, not because
a binary field was too narrow. That is the methodological gain: new premises
can be added \emph{as themselves}, and their consequences proved rather than
suggested by renaming.

The binary development is not retracted. It remains a correct special case:
when a kind is exclusive and dichotomous, settlement orients one value and
excludes the other (\lean{dichotomous\_orients}). Cadence and swirl can be
reintroduced later as optional structure on a strand, not as the engine of
every kind.

\section{Check}

The Lean source imports only \texttt{Init}. Under Lean~4.24.0:

\begin{verbatim}
elan run leanprover/lean4:v4.24.0 lean OpenSite.lean
\end{verbatim}

A successful check exits without output. The file contains no
\texttt{axiom}, \texttt{sorry}, \texttt{admit}, or \texttt{native\_decide}.
