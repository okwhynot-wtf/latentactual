\begin{abstract}
A step is already ordinal, so a metaphysics that takes acts as primitive
gets time almost free and has nowhere for extension to come from. This
note starts from which determinations can be settled together. Locality
is amalgamation, and it holds exactly when coherence is pairwise.
Actualisation is determined exactly as far as coherence forces it; the
free residue is not fillable by any function of the configuration.
Time is the ancestral of drawing and settling; revision does not
advance. Lean~4 checks the load-bearing claims in
\texttt{OpenSite.lean}.
\end{abstract}

\section{The diagnosis}

The binary latent--actual development, and the first draft of Open Sites,
both took a step as primitive. Seed, cadence, offer, rank, and
\lean{Continues.persists} are elaborations of that sequential peg.
\lean{Continues.persists} stipulated that an act disturbs one kind, which
is exactly the clause that prevents spatial content: nothing in the
primitive said which determinations constrain which.

The test is: for each target notion, complete ``$X$ would be ---, in
terms of the primitives.'' If the sentence has no completion, the notion
will not be derived. Under an act-first schema, ``space would be ---''
has none. Under compossibility it completes: space is the structure of
what can be settled together.

The binary file is not retracted. It remains the right special case for
promote-and-turn on dichotomous kinds. This note is the successor schema.

\section{Ceiling}

What holds, holds together. A configuration is an admissible partial
assignment of values to kinds, together with an articulation predicate.
Single-valuedness needs no exclusivity axiom: an assignment maps each
kind to at most one value.

Space is independence and interaction in pairwise compatibility.
Locality is amalgamation of independent settlements, and that theorem
is the content of taking compossibility to be pairwise, not a free
lunch. Time is the ancestral of drawing a kind and settling an open
one. Oscillation is revision at fixed articulation: it adds nothing,
so it does not advance. The possible is unused coherence at an open
kind. Observation is restriction to a region of kinds.

Coherence can actualise only by narrowing. Where one value remains
admissible, the kind is forced. Where two remain, it is free, and no
selector or configuration-reading policy is respected there.

Out of scope: dimension, metric, signature, phenomenal duration,
embodiment, consciousness, being-from-nothing, and $\omega$-history
from seriality.

\section{The schema}

\begin{definition}[Ontology]
Kinds are types of values. Binary opposition is a property some kinds
may have, not the shape of the field.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Coherence]
A pairwise compatibility relation on determinations, reflexive and
symmetric.
\end{definition}

A configuration is an admissible partial assignment together with an
articulation predicate. Articulation accumulates; values vary.

\begin{definition}[Independence]
Kinds $k$ and $\ell$ are independent when every pair of values is
compatible.
\end{definition}

\begin{formalresult}[Locality is a theorem of pairwise coherence]
If $k$ and $\ell$ are independent and both are open at $\sigma$, then
coherent settlements of each amalgamate to a coherent joint settlement,
the two orders of update agree, and what is settable at $\ell$ is
unchanged by settling $k$.
\end{formalresult}

This is \lean{independent\_amalgamate}, \lean{independent\_commute},
and \lean{independent\_settable\_iff}. The world model witnesses the
complement: switch-off and lamp-on are each admissible and jointly not
(\lean{interaction\_blocks\_amalgamation}).

\begin{formalresult}[Locality needs pairwise generation]
There is a downward-closed admissibility that is not pairwise: any two
of three kinds may be settled positively, all three may not. Those
kinds are compatible on every pair of values taken by themselves, each
is separately settleable given the third, and they fail to amalgamate
(\lean{locality\_needs\_pairwise}). So amalgamation is not derived from
``being a constraint''; it is the content of taking constraints to be
pairs. A Borromean world has no locality in this sense, and no amount
of care about acts would restore it.
\end{formalresult}

\begin{definition}[Passages]
Settling fills an open kind. Growing articulates a new kind, same
assignment. Revising changes a value, same articulation.
\end{definition}

\begin{formalresult}[The arrow is drawing and settling]
\lean{Advances} is monotone articulation, persistence of settled
values, and something newly drawn or newly settled.
\lean{Earlier} is the ancestral of growth and settlement. It is
irreflexive and asymmetric. Revision draws nothing and settles nothing
that was open, so it is no advance in either direction
(\lean{revises\_not\_advances}).
\end{formalresult}

The previous definition counted only drawing. Then a settlement and
its result were unordered in both directions, and everything that
happened to values happened outside time. Settling now moves the
archive (\lean{settles\_earlier}, \lean{World.settling\_advances}).
The world model revises hue from red to green without advancing
(\lean{World.hue\_oscillates}).

\begin{formalresult}[Forcing, or how far coherence actualises]
An open kind is \lean{Forced} when exactly one value remains
settable, \lean{Free} when two or more remain. Forced kinds can be
settled only one way. A respected selector at a forced kind is pinned
to that value: it records what the constraints left
(\lean{forced\_pins\_selector}). At a free kind, even a policy that
reads the whole configuration is refuted, because plenitude supplies
two settlements from the same source (\lean{free\_refutes\_policy}).
Hence \lean{selection\_is\_redundant\_or\_refuted}: if a selection is
respected at a settleable open kind, that kind was already forced.
\end{formalresult}

The world model witnesses both sides. With the lamp on, the switch is
forced to on (\lean{World.sw\_forced\_by\_lamp}). The hue at the origin
is free, so no policy is respected there
(\lean{World.no\_policy\_at\_hue}).

Plenitude at an open kind is the constructors: every settable value has
a settle. Coherence alone does not yield a successor
(\lean{productivity\_is\_not\_coherence\_level}). Observation is
restriction of an assignment to a region of kinds --- veridical
access, not consciousness. Illusion is a further primitive.

\section{Models}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \lean{Void}: null assignment, remainder. Presentation structure
        does not create actuality
        (\lean{nullity\_not\_derivable}).
  \item \lean{Borromean}: three-way admissibility, not pairwise.
        Pairwise-looking independence fails to amalgamate.
  \item \lean{World}: hue, gap, switch, and lamp. Hue amalgamates with
        switch; switch-off and lamp-on do not. Lamp-on forces the
        switch; hue at the origin is free. Seed grows to origin.
        Hue revises without advancing.
\end{itemize}

\section{Objections}

The binary paper answered thirteen objections in a flat list. Several
are retired (binary-only field, cadence as time, internally marked
past). The rest group by what they target.

\subsection{Method}

\paragraph{The conclusions are stipulated in the premises.}
Pairwise compatibility is a primitive. The reply is a dependency
audit. Locality is a theorem of that primitive, not of acts, and it
fails when admissibility is not pairwise. Selection, if respected, is
implied by forcing; if the kind is free, it is refuted.

\paragraph{The models are artificial.}
They are, in the proper sense of model constructions. Void, Borromean,
and World catch packages that do not follow: actuality from
presentation, locality without pairs, selection at a free kind.

\subsection{The field}

\paragraph{Binary opposition is metaphysically reductive.}
Kinds are types of values. Hue has three. The reductive charge against
the field as a whole no longer applies.

\paragraph{Contrary determination has been confused with logical
negation.}
An assignment is a function, so a kind carries at most one value.
That is not $\neg$. Compatibility between distinct determinations is a
further pairwise fact, not logical denial.

\paragraph{Pairwise compatibility is too weak; the world has
Borromean constraints.}
It is too weak for those constraints, and that is now a theorem
(\lean{locality\_needs\_pairwise}). A metaphysics that needs
irreducibly three-way incompatibility must add it and give up
amalgamation.

\subsection{Latency and possibility}

\paragraph{Latency is merely a negative predicate.}
Unset is non-assignment. Open, settled, and undrawn are distinguished
by articulation. What is not supplied is an intensional theory of
powers or degrees.

\paragraph{Plenitude is too strong.}
There is no maximality axiom. The constructors supply a settle for
every settable value at an open kind. The remaining strength is
counting those as possible, which is what refutes a policy at a free
kind.

\paragraph{The non-nullity argument is circular.}
There is no non-nullity argument. \lean{Void} is admissible.

\subsection{Time}

\paragraph{The archive simply stipulates the arrow of time.}
\lean{Earlier} is defined as the ancestral of drawing and settling.
Irreflexivity is a theorem of \lean{Advances}. The theory does not
derive an arrow from laws that do not mention growth or settlement.

\paragraph{Recurrence is not return.}
Along \lean{Earlier}, a chain cannot return to its source. Revision
can restore a previous value and is no advance. The honest slogan:
drawing and settling do not return; revision at fixed articulation
can.

\paragraph{This is not lived time.}
Correct. No cadence, duration, or internally marked past.

\subsection{Space}

\paragraph{Independence is not space.}
It is not dimension or metric. It is which kinds constrain which.
Adjacency and commuting updates, yes; relativistic spacetime, no.

\paragraph{Independence is smuggled as a law of kinds.}
It is not. Switch and lamp interact in World; hue does not interact
with switch.

\subsection{Actuality and observation}

\paragraph{A selector makes determinacy trivial.}
Where the kind is forced, the selector is redundant. Where it is
free, no selector or policy is respected. Determinacy is exactly the
forcing; there is no further posit.

\paragraph{Restriction does not explain experience.}
It does not. Restriction is veridical access to a region of kinds.
Illusion is extra.

\section{Scope}

Adjacency, independence, commuting updates, forcing, and a strict
order of drawing and settling, yes. Dimension, metric, phenomenal
duration, embodiment, and $\omega$-history, no.

What is carried over: unlabelled (now un-$n$-ary) kinds, unused
coherence as the unique existential, internal articulation from the
start. What is rejected: a binary-only field; acts as the spatial
primitive; selection as an independent actualiser; locality without
pairwise generation.
